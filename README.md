## 📈 Key Insights

**Q: What is the total revenue and total boxes shipped?**
A: $27.99M in total revenue, from 2M boxes shipped, across Jan 2023 – Sep 2024.

**Q: What is the top shipping destination by revenue?**
A: UK, generating $7.66M (27.36% of total revenue), followed by USA at $6.28M (22.43%) and India at $4.58M (16.37%). Canada, New Zealand, and Australia follow behind.

**Q: What is the top-performing product by revenue?**
A: Orange Choco, followed closely by Drinking Coco and 99% Dark & Pure. Organic Choco S... and Peanut Butter Cu... round out the top 5.

**Q: Which sales person generated the most revenue?**
A: Brien Boise, with $2.17M in total sales, the highest among all reps listed. Dotty Strutley ($1.67M) and Barr Faughny ($1.25M) follow.

**Q: How did monthly revenue trend over time?**
A: Revenue fluctuated month to month rather than growing steadily. Notable peaks appear around Apr 2023, Jul 2023, and Apr 2024 (each nearing or exceeding 1.5M), while dips occur in early 2023 and early 2024 (dropping closer to 1.0M).

**Q: What does the date filter allow?**
A: A slicer range from 1/2/2023 to 9/30/2024, letting the report be narrowed to any custom time window.
